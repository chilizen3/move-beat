## About

### Fun hobbies, but no moving

Many people want to enjoy their hobbies more. Moving your body can feel like a chore. Move Beat makes it feel like a game.

- Idea: a rhythm game you play with your whole body
- One short round lasts just one minute
- Neon night arcade look: navy, teal and yellow
- Playable in English or Japanese

### How Move Beat works

Move icons fall down three lanes. Do the move, then tap the matching big button when the icon hits the yellow line.

- Three moves: Jump, Hands up and Squat
- Tap the big button with the same icon
- Judgements: Perfect, Great, Good or Miss
- Score, combo and rank show your run

### A silly reward, then again

After one minute, you see a funny comparison, like how many fried chicken pieces you moved off. It is just for fun.

- Result: karaage comparison, about kcal and steps
- Clear note: not a real calorie count
- Best score is saved on your device
- Next: decide who the app is for
